# fly2israel — Research Log
## True Label Data Collection

### Methodology
Cross-reference data from: (1) Wikipedia destination pages, (2) reputable news sources (Reuters, BBC, eTurboNews, Times of Israel), (3) airline official announcements.

https://eturbonews.com/international-carriers-resume-israel-flights-in-2024/
